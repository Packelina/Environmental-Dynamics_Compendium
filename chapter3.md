# Chapter 3: Estuaries and coastal zones - nurseries and graveyards of the aquatic system

**The coastal zone accounts for only 20 percent of the global area. However, this region is one of the most important and indeed fragile parts of the world, both ecologically and for human civilization. The fraction of people living within a distance of 100 km from the coast is estimated to reach 80 percent by 2010 {cite:p}`jonsson2005`. Rivers and their discharge points into the oceans have always been important settlement sites, providing a natural meeting place between the sea and the inland.**
