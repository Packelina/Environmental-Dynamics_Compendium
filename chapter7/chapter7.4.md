(sec-7-4)=
# 7.4. Case Studies

As already mentioned, material flow analysis is a tool that can be used in e.g. Environmental Management and Engineering, Industrial Ecology, Resource Management, Waste Management, Changes in Amount and Composition of Wastes etc. The following three examples show how different the data stored in and provided by MFA can be used for various purposes.

(sec-7-4-1)=
## 7.4.1. Municipal MFA: Community Box

The first case study is about a simple flow accounting model on a regional level. The approach considers the entire municipal metabolism of different materials, i.e. both anthropogenic and environmental flows are recorded. The temporal boundary is set to one year to enable policy making based on yearly accounts. The example below shows reactive nitrogen flows [t/yr] through the municipality of Stockholm consisting of various anthropogenic sectors that use, produce or transform reactive nitrogen components and natural compartments such as air, water and soil. (@burstrom1998)

(sec-7-4-2)=
## 7.4.2. Input-output analysis of nitrogen flows in the Norrström drainage basin

The second case study illustrates the use of input-output flow analysis (based on MFA) as a tool for quantification of coupled natural and cross-sectoral flows of water, nutrients, and pollutants in catchments. The study quantifies implications of various environmental regulation and management scenarios for both natural water systems and engineered-economic systems and sectors that use and impact natural waters for meeting human needs. For the Norrström drainage basin results indicate considerable nitrogen load contributions to surface and coastal waters from slow groundwater flow paths and legacies of accumulated nitrogen in subsurface and immobile water pools. This implies that effective nitrogen load abatement cannot focus only on active sources but must also include downstream measures, which can capture and abate nitrogen/pollutant loading from different types of known and yet unknown point and diffuse sources within associated catchments.

The figure below shows the initial MFA of nitrogen flows among the different water using and impacting sectors of the drainage basin as well as imports and export. The figure further indicates the level of uncertainty of the various reported flow data. (@baresel2005b)
