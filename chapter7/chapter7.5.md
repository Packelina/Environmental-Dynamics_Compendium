# 7.5. Future strategies - sustainable development

There is an increasing interest in investigating material flows through society and the environment. MFA is useful because it can help to link anthropogenic activities with resource consumption and environmental loadings. Therefore, we may say that MFA will become a major design tool to optimize the metabolism of the anthroposphere directly or indirectly by environmental policies and legislations.

The present metabolism of the anthroposphere is not sustainable as it is to unidirectional: it produces too much emission while it uses too many resources. The lack of appropriate sinks within the anthroposphere and the environments therefore requires a more resource-oriented metabolism of the anthroposphere. It is important to remember that recycling only provides an intermediate solution as it prolongs the residence time of materials in the anthroposphere. Modeling material flows through the anthroposphere will help us to redesign our present anthropogenic metabolism for a more sustainable future.
