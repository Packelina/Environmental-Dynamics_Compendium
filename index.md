# Preface

While affiliated with Water Resources Engineering (Vattenvårdsteknik, KTH), a number of individuals, apart from the authors, have contributed to the development of the material that is included in this compendium for the course Environmental Dynamics: Physical Processes. In here we wish to acknowledge these contributions and also specifically note the contributions by the current authors.

The foundation of the course was laid down by Vladimir Cvetkovic and Jan-Olof Selroos during the first three years of the course (1993-1995). In the next phase, Jerker Järsjö contributed by restructuring and expanding the problems/exercises of Subsurface water and Surface water parts. A particular contribution in the period 1995-1997 was done by Thomas Larm who developed exercises for the Ecosystem/biosphere part focusing on eutrophication of lakes and phosphor limited processes; Sally Salmon further expanded these exercises at a later stage. Carmen Prieto contributed to the exercises for the subsurface/groundwater part, in particular by including geostatistical examples. Gia Destouni and Peter Rogberg provided the first structure and content for the atmosphere/climate part.

A significant upgrade of the course material took place in the period 2000-2004 due to Georg Lindgren and Christian Baresel. Georg Lindgren contributed significantly to improving and expanding the surface water part of the course. Christian Baresel expanded and restructured the atmosphere part with focus on climate, and also expanded the subsurface part of the course.

Until 2004, the course lecture material was available in form of handwritten notes as developed by Vladimir Cvetkovic. The exercises however had been available as handouts as part of the development noted above. Since 2004 significant effort was directed toward developing full and independent material for the course, integrating both lectures and problems/exercises.


The contributions to creating the present version of the compendium since 2004 can be summarized as follows:

- **Chapter 1 (Conceptual basis):** Philippe Martinet wrote the bulk of the material based on existing handwritten lecture notes (Vladimir Cvetkovic) retaining the basic structure but expanding the discussions, examples (though not fully implemented yet) and explanations significantly.
- **Chapter 2 (Streams, lakes and drainage basins):** The material was prepared by Georg Lindgren who made a significant expansion of previous content by adding the text on catchment-scale hydrological modeling and the Norrström example.
- **Chapter 3 (Estuaries and coastal zones):** The material was prepared by Christoffer Carstens who also wrote and compiled the final version.
- **Chapter 4 (Soil and groundwater):** The final version was written and compiled by Staffan Molin based on lecture notes and the expanded exercises as developed by Christian Baresel
- **Chapter 5 (Biosphere):** The final text was written by Aleksandra Nikolic based on lecture notes and including additional material.
- **Chapter 6 (Climate change):** The text was created by Christian Baresel based on the material compiled earlier by himself.
- **Chapter 7 (Antropogenic metabolism):** The text was created by Christian Baresel based on the material compiled earlier by himself.

As a final note, I would like to emphasize that the entire compendium is available as a dynamic Web-based text written in XML; we believe it is the first such comprehensive course material at KTH prepared in XML. The effort toward preparing the text in XML was significant. The main architect for the XML version is Philippe Martinet whose unique programming skills and dedication have laid the foundation for the Web-based interactive version of the compendium. Furthermore, Philippe Martinet's work inspired Christian Baresel and Staffan Molin who themselves mastered XML programming and contributed significantly to the completion of the XML version of the course material.
