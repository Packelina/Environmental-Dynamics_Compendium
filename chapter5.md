# Chapter 5: The biosphere: On ecosystems, global cycles and eutrophication

The biosphere is a part of the earth (surface layer of land with water bodies, the oceans and sediments) and atmosphere in which life exists (all species). Humans live in a part called technosphere or anthroposphere. The ecosystem is a part where humans do not live, and is made up of a community of animal, plants and microorganisms like viruses, bacteria.
